Human Hungry Hippos in an inflatable! Tethered to a bungee, you launch into the center ball pit, scoop up as many balls as your arms can carry, and get yanked back to your corner. Most balls when the pit runs dry wins — absolute chaos.

Every team member rides down and back fully positioned on the inner tube the whole way. First team to get all its members through, tube and all, takes the win!

The five D’s: dodge, duck, dip, dive, and dodge. Two teams, a line of balls down the middle, zero mercy — last team with players standing wins.

Sink a putt, get a point! Want a little handicap? Take a drink to move your ball closer to the hole. ⛳🍺

The classic. Partner up and toss the water balloon back and forth, taking a step back after every catch. Last pair with an unbroken balloon wins — splash zone guaranteed! 💦

The pretzel classic. Spin the dial, contort onto the mat, and hold your spot — last one who doesn’t topple into a tangle of limbs wins.

The classic backyard drinking game. Two teams, bottles in the middle — peg the other team’s bottle with a ball, then chug before they reset it and run it back. Aim true, drink fast.
